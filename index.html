Quoridor Corner Puzzles

Corner Puzzles

A mini puzzle game spun off from Kyutae Lee's Quoridor AI (4-player adaptation).

Goal: rearrange the four pawns in the marked 2x2 corner block into the target arrangement shown at the side of the board.

Rules:
- Turn order is fixed: Yellow → Black → Blue → Green; every pawn moves every turn
- Moves: step to an adjacent cell; jump straight over an adjacent pawn;
  diagonal jump beside a pawn when the straight jump is impossible
- No walls, and no pawn may ever land on its own goal line
  (the colored bars at the board edges show whose goal line it is)
- Moves stay inside the un-shaded corner region
- The pawn starting on the corner cell always moves first

Hint: the hint button re-solves the puzzle from the current position (provably shortest) and shows the next best move. Par = the optimal move count.

Stars: 3 for par, 2 within par+2, otherwise 1.
