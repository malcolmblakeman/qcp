Quoridor Corner Puzzles

Corner Puzzles

A mini puzzle game spun off from Kyutae Lee's Quoridor AI (4-player adaptation).

Goal

Rearrange the four pawns in the marked 2x2 corner block into the target arrangement shown beside the board.

Rules

  • Turn Order: Fixed: Yellow 🟡 → Red 🔴 → Blue 🔵 → Green 🟢. Every pawn moves every turn.
  • Moves: Step to an adjacent cell, jump straight over an adjacent pawn, or diagonal jump beside a pawn if the straight jump is blocked.
  • No Walls: Unlike standard Quoridor, there are no walls here.
  • Goal Lines: No pawn may ever land on its own goal line (the colored bars at the board edges).
  • Region: Moves must stay inside the un-shaded corner region.
  • First Move: The pawn starting on the corner cell always moves first.

Controls

🔄Restart: Reset the current puzzle.
↩️Undo: Take back your last move.
🎲Random: Load a random puzzle.
💡Hint: Shows the next best move (provably shortest). Par = optimal move count.
🔥Streak: Endless mode. Solve chained puzzles without failing!
📍Mode: Cycles between Normal, Round-Trip (↔️), and Grand Tour (🗺️). In Streak mode, cycles difficulty (🟢🟡🔴📈).
ℹ️About: Opens this info panel.

Modes & Scoring

  • Stars: 3 stars for matching par, 2 within par+2, otherwise 1 star.
  • Round Trip (↔️): Solve to the target, then solve it back to the start.
  • Grand Tour (🗺️): Visit all 6 target arrangements in one run. Click any of the 6 mini-cards to start the tour from there.

Streak mode

Solve chained random puzzles. Your streak grows with every solve.
